Computer Vision Intern

As a Computer Vision Intern at VastWorks, you will work on state-of-the-art visual perception pipelines and multimodal models designed to perceive, analyze, and act on visual enterprise data in real time.

What You Will Build

  • High-throughput video stream analytics and visual feature extraction pipelines.
  • Real-time object detection, semantic segmentation, and temporal spatial tracking models.
  • Multimodal reasoning experiments combining Vision-Language-Action Models (VLAMs) with autonomous agent workflows.
  • Inference optimization pipelines for sub-millisecond edge and GPU deployment.

What We Look For

  • Strong proficiency in Python, PyTorch, and OpenCV.
  • Solid grasp of foundational computer vision architectures (YOLO, Vision Transformers, self-supervised models).
  • Hands-on experience training, fine-tuning, or optimizing neural networks on custom visual datasets.
  • High agency, fast execution velocity, and hunger to build production-grade enterprise AI.
