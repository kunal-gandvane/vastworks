Computer Vision Intern

Position: Open Location: Remote Eligibility: IIT Bombay Only Term: Winter Internship

About VastWorks

VastWorks builds AI systems for enterprise workflow automation covering computer vision, agent-based automation, and retrieval systems built around real operational problems. We’re an early-stage team based out of IIT Bombay, working on applied AI with a focus on practical, demonstrable results over generic tooling.

About the Role

We’re looking for a Computer Vision Intern to join our team for this winter. You’ll work directly on computer vision projects covering areas like object detection, image classification, and related CV pipelines contributing to real systems we’re building and showcasing. This is hands-on, project-based work where you’ll get to apply what you know (or are learning) to a live, evolving product.

What You’ll Do

  • Work on computer vision projects end-to-end from data handling to model building to evaluation
  • Collaborate closely with the founding team and other interns
  • Get exposure to how a CV system is built and deployed in a real-world, enterprise-facing context
  • Contribute to projects that will be showcased as part of VastWorks’ portfolio

Eligibility

  • B.Tech or Dual Degree students
  • IIT Bombay students only
  • 1st, 2nd, or 3rd year students
  • No prior experience required but relevant coursework, projects, or exposure to CV/ML is appreciated
  • Genuine interest in computer vision and a willingness to learn are what matter most

What We’re Looking For

  • Curiosity and willingness to learn computer vision concepts and tools
  • Basic programming familiarity (Python preferred)
  • Ability to commit a few hours a week consistently through the winter break
  • Bonus: any prior exposure to ML/CV through coursework, SoC, clubs, or personal projects not required, but a plus

Details

  • Duration: Winter Internship (dates flexible, to be finalized with selected interns)
  • Mode: Remote
  • What you’ll gain: Hands-on project experience, direct mentorship, a certificate of completion, and a real project to add to your portfolio/resume
